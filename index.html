Ivaylo Ivanov

Game developer

I am Ivaylo, a game developer, and I am building my first commercial game.

I have more than 3 years of professional experience as a game developer in Unity/C# and C++. I was part of the team behind the MMORPG Scars of Honor. I built client- and server-side systems like input, achievements and trading, and ported early builds to Android. As a freelancer I built gameplay for an RTS, a 2D platformer and consulted on a story-driven game of minigames.

Before games, I worked in automation and site reliability, so I like building stable systems and tools as much as games.
